# The image bank

Plates (background images) we own, sorted by job. **Source from here first**; generate
only for gaps, and bank every keeper so the library compounds. Nothing in the bank
contains text, letters or logos.

## The style bar (filled by the brand skill from docs/wiki/brand/visual.md)
