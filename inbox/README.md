# inbox: drop anything here

Put anything about your business in this folder: I read it, file it safely, and learn
from it, so you never have to explain the same thing twice. Nothing here is ever deleted;
originals are kept in the project notebook.

**What you can drop**
- Documents: PDFs, Word files, slides, notes, flyers, price lists
- Past conversations with an AI (ChatGPT, Claude...): export or copy-paste into a .txt file
- Photos and screenshots (Instagram posts, your old website, things you like)
- Videos and voice recordings (reels, talks, voice notes)
- Website links: put them in a file called `links.txt`, one link per line

**Instagram posts**
- Public posts: just put the post link in `links.txt`.
- Private posts or stories: take screenshots and put each post in its own folder, slides
  numbered in order (`my-launch-post/01.png`, `02.png` ...).

**How to upload (in the browser, on github.com)**
1. Open your project on GitHub and click the `inbox` folder.
2. Click **Add file → Upload files**.
3. Drag your files in (or click "choose your files").
4. Click the green **Commit changes** button at the bottom.
Then tell me in the chat: "I put new files in the inbox."

You can also simply attach files in the chat; I'll save them here myself.
Very large videos (over 100 MB) cannot go to GitHub: send a shorter clip or a link.
