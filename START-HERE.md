# Start here

This folder is your studio. You talk to one assistant, **Nova**: your strategic co-founder,
who also runs the studio for you. Nova wants to understand your whole business, brings ideas,
asks the hard questions, and once a month steps back with you for a "board meeting". Nova starts where you are, with your idea, and
works through it with you until it is a business you can explain, sell and build. Then
Nova builds your website and, if you need it, your app and dashboard. Behind the scenes
Nova brings in specialists (research, strategy, writing, design, building) and checks
their work before showing it to you.

**You never need to know what to ask.** Nova always suggests the next step, one question
at a time, and you decide. You never need to write code either.

## The five studios

Everything you ask goes to one of five studios. Nova tells you which one in a small line
at the top of each answer, like `▸ Business studio · Offer and pricing`.

| Studio | For |
|---|---|
| **Business** (we start here) | your idea, your clients, your offer and prices, what could go wrong, your first 90 days |
| **Marketing & SEO** | being found on Google and in AI answers, your message, your launch |
| **Website & design** | your look, your pages, your website |
| **App & dashboard** | logins, a client area, bookings, your own back office |
| **Content** | posts, carousels, videos for social media |

## One-time setup (about 15 minutes, ideally with the person who shared this with you)

1. **Make your own private copy.** On this page on GitHub, click **Use this template** →
   **Create a new repository**. Give it a name (for example `my-studio`), choose
   **Private**, and click **Create repository**. Private matters: your copy will hold your
   business ideas, prices and files. Only this empty template is public.
2. **Open Claude.** Go to **claude.ai/code**, connect your GitHub account when asked, and
   pick your new repository.
3. **Environment.** In the environment settings (the environment name at the top of
   the session, then **Edit**), set **Network access** so the studio can reach the
   web: Instagram, your old website, and the tools it installs.
4. **First message.** Type: **"Hi Nova, let's start."** Nova takes it from there.

Later, when you reach the website and the app, Nova asks you to connect
**Vercel** (hosting) and **Supabase** (database). You connect them in claude.ai under
**Settings → Connectors**. Nova tells you when.

## Every day

- **Start a conversation:** say what you want, or just "continue".
- **Send Nova files:** old conversations, PDFs, screenshots, Instagram posts, videos,
  voice notes. Attach them in the chat, or upload them into the `inbox` folder on
  GitHub (**Add file → Upload files**). For links, paste them in the chat.
- **Answer Nova's questions.** One at a time, always with a suggested answer. "Yes", "the second one" or "not sure, you decide" are all fine.
- **Nothing goes public without your "publish".** You get a preview link first.

## When Nova says "let's start a new conversation"

Long conversations make any AI slower and less sharp, so Nova keeps an eye on this for
you. When Nova asks, open a **new conversation** on the same repository and
type **"continue"**. Nova has already saved where you were, so nothing is lost.

## Where things live (you rarely need this)

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `inbox/` | Anything you want the studio to read |
| `docs/wiki/` | The studio's memory: your business, brand, website, decisions |
| `handoffs/` | A note at the end of each conversation: where we stopped |
| `web/` | Your website and app (created when we build it) |
| `motion/` | The engine for animated slides and videos |
| `content/` | Your posts, post types and image bank |

## If something goes wrong

Tell Nova what you saw, in your own words. A screenshot helps. If it is
stuck, Nova says so plainly and tells you what to send the person who set this up with you.
