# Website: from the business pages to a live site

Inputs that must exist first: `business/product-spec.md` (signed off), `business/offer.md`, `business/audience.md`,
`business/positioning.md`, `business/funnel.md` (confirmed), `brand/voice.md` and
`brand/visual.md` (at least draft), and the sitemap seed in `docs/wiki/website/_index.md`.
Missing? Business studio modules (`.claude/studios/business.md`) and `brand` first; a site built on guesses is rebuilt.

This is a T3 project: run `kickoff`, which writes `docs/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-website.md` with
one step per conversation.

## 0 · Plan: page map and journeys (Website & design module 2)

Before any design: turn `business/product-spec.md` (and the page map in
`marketing/seo-plan.md`, if it exists) into the page table in `docs/wiki/website/_index.md`
(`page · URL · job · call-to-action · status`). Then the **ux-designer** writes
`docs/wiki/website/journeys.md`: the two or three paths that matter (a stranger finds her and
becomes a lead; a lead becomes a client; a client comes back), each as numbered steps with
the page, what the visitor needs to see there, and the next click. Read both back to her in
plain words; her yes closes module 2.

## 1 · Stack (one message, max 3 questions)

Default: **Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind) + Supabase + Vercel**: one codebase
grows from site to web app to dashboard, previews for every change, free tiers to start,
and it is the stack the kit's specialists know best. Confirm or override with her:

1. "Will people log in one day (clients, members, a course)? I'd plan for yes."
2. "Do you already pay for a website tool (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress) you want to keep?"
3. "Do you need to sell online (payments, bookings)? Which tools do you use for that today?"

Override only for a real reason (e.g. she must stay on an existing platform). Record the
choice in `docs/wiki/decisions/_index.md` with the reason, and in `docs/wiki/website/_index.md`.

## 2 · Scaffold (once; **builder** does it)

```bash
npx create-next-app@latest web --ts --tailwind --app --eslint --no-src-dir --import-alias "@/*" --use-npm --yes
```

- Everything lives in `web/`. Add `web/config/launch.ts`: **the launch switch**, one file
  that decides which pages, menu items and buttons are live. "Is X live?" is answered
  there, never by whether a page file exists.
- Design tokens (colours, fonts, spacing) from `brand/visual.md` go into the Tailwind
  config and global CSS once; **designer** owns them. Pages never hardcode colours.
- Connect hosting: she connects the **Vercel** connector (claude.ai → Settings →
  Connectors) when this step starts. Create the Vercel project from the GitHub repo with
  **Root Directory = `web`** and **Production Branch = `live`** (Project → Settings → Environments/Git;
  Vercel defaults to `main`, so change it when you create the project, before the first push).
  Every pushed branch then gets a **preview link** she can open on her phone; the `main`
  preview is her stable "work in progress" link (record it in `state.md`); `live` is the live site.
- Domain: later, at the first publish; she buys or connects it in Vercel with your step-by-step help.

## 3 · Build page by page

For each row of the sitemap table, follow `.claude/sops/01-new-page.md`:
copy first (**copywriter**, from the wiki, her words) → her OK on the words → the
approved mockup (`web-design` skill; `docs/design/mockups/approved/`) → **designer** +
**builder** → **verifier** → screenshots at **390 px and 1440 px** (designer) → she looks at
the preview link → change or approve. One or two pages per conversation.

Every page:
- ends with the **standard offer** from `business/funnel.md`, same name, same button, everywhere;
- has its own title (under 60 characters), description (under 160), canonical URL and
  social image (a page without its own `<head>` inherits the home page's);
- works on a phone first (tap targets, no hover-only effects, readable without zoom).

Keep `docs/wiki/website/_index.md` current: a table `page · URL · job · status`
(`planned · draft · preview · live`) plus the stack, the launch-switch file
(`web/config/launch.ts`), the Vercel project name and the live URL.

## 4 · Search and AI-answer visibility (SEO / GEO)

- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` generated by Next (`app/sitemap.ts`, `app/robots.ts`).
  After deploying, **check their content** on the preview, not just that they load:
  some setups answer any unknown URL with the home page and status 200.
- Structured data (JSON-LD) only for true claims: business name, address, offers, the
  author. Never a credential, rating or award that is not real for that page and person.
- One topic per page, the visitor's question in the heading, a plain answer right under it.

## 5 · Articles (journal, guides, blog)

How articles are written, reviewed and published is the `article-writing` skill (Marketing &
SEO studio). One standard, one place. What the **site** must give every article:
- One articles folder and one route pattern, decided once and recorded in
  `docs/wiki/website/_index.md` (the writing skills look it up there).
- Its own `<head>`: title, description, canonical, real published and updated dates.
- `Article` structured data with the real author, and `FAQPage` built from the article's
  question headings and their standalone answers (only questions actually on the page).
- An author box with real credentials only, the sources list, and the free next step.
- Listed in `sitemap.xml`; drafts and stubs `noindex` and out of the sitemap.
- The closed set of article blocks as components, nothing styled ad hoc.

## 6 · Legal pages

Imprint/legal notice, privacy policy, cookie notice (and terms if she sells online). Draft
them from her business data with **copywriter**, clearly marked as drafts, and tell her
plainly: "These are a starting point. Please have them checked by a professional for your
country before the site goes live." Only add a cookie banner if the site really sets
non-essential cookies or trackers.

## 7 · Publish

`.claude/sops/03-publish.md`, every time. She says "publish" → publish `main` to `live` → check the
live URL on a phone width → update `website/_index.md` (status `live`), `state.md` (live
links), `log.md`. Unpublished branch work is not live, whatever the notes say.

## Done for the phase

All sitemap pages `live`, standard offer on every page, legal pages checked by her
professional (or flagged as waiting in `state.md`), domain connected. Then `state.md` →
Website & design ☑ in the map, next: App & dashboard (if the spec has an app) and a handoff.
