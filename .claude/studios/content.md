# Content studio

**Purpose:** social posts, carousels, short videos and images, in her brand. Runs alongside
the other studios once `docs/wiki/brand/` exists. **Owns:** `content/`, `docs/wiki/content/`.
**Specialists:** `copywriter`, `content-producer`, `media-analyst`, `designer`.

## Playbook
| She wants | Skill |
|---|---|
| A post, carousel or reel; edits to one | `carousel` |
| "I want something like this" (someone else's post) | `inspiration` |
| You to watch or read a video, reel, screenshots | `media-reading` |

## Rules
- Copy is locked before the visuals; `node content/check-copy.mjs` before anything ships.
- Every post ends on her free standard offer (`business/offer.md`) unless she says otherwise.
